# RAG Test Questions

## Contextual Questions (answerable from the documents)

1. **What was the murder weapon in "Lamb to the Slaughter" and how did Mary dispose of it?**
   A frozen leg of lamb. She put it in the oven to cook, then persuaded the investigating police officers to eat it for supper — destroying the evidence while they searched the house for a heavy blunt instrument.

2. **What special property did the swindlers claim their fabric possessed in "The Emperor's New Clothes"?**
   The clothes made from the fabric would be invisible to anyone who was either unfit for their position or intolerably stupid.

3. **What was the bet between the two brothers in "The £1,000,000 Bank-Note"?**
   Brother A said an honest, intelligent stranger given nothing but a million-pound note would starve because he could never cash or spend it without being arrested. Brother B bet twenty thousand pounds that the man would survive thirty days and stay out of jail.

4. **Why did Mary Maloney go to the grocery store after killing her husband?**
   To establish an alibi. She rehearsed her manner and voice in the mirror, then went to buy potatoes and peas from Sam the grocer, chatting normally about cooking supper for Patrick — so she could return home and "discover" the body as if she'd been out shopping.

5. **How did Henry Adams end up in London penniless?**
   He was a mining-broker's clerk in San Francisco. One Saturday he ventured too far in his sailboat and was carried out to sea. He was picked up by a brig bound for London and made to work his passage without pay. He arrived with ragged clothes and only a dollar, which lasted him 24 hours.
